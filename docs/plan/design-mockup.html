Graphite & Citron

The interface is a design document on warm paper, set in graphite and reviewed with a highlighter. Colour carries meaning here (strong, partial and gap evidence, failure events, AI content), so the chrome carries none. Primary buttons are ink. Citron is the only brand hue, used like a highlighter on what is new, selected or most important. It is the one hue that can't be confused with a signal or with AI.

Headlines are set in Newsreader, so pages read like an argued document. Geist handles the tool and Geist Mono the labels and numbers. Diagrams stay the hero, now drawn on a dot-grid pad. AI output looks like pencil on vellum: a violet-tinted surface with a dashed edge, always labelled, and provisional until you ink it in.
